# Conversation: A gym consultation where Trent is debating whether to cancel his membership or stay committed

Participants: Marcus Delgado, Renata Osei, Trent Kowalczyk

**Marcus Delgado:** Sit down, Trent. You look like a man about to quit on himself. Talk. What's going on in that head.

**Renata Osei:** Marcus, let's not put it that way — Trent's not quitting on himself just because he's sitting here weighing things. That's him taking it seriously, actually. Trent, how are you doing, really? Not the gym stuff first — just you.

**Trent Kowalczyk:** Me? I'm fine. Look, I'm not here for a therapy session, I'm here because I've been coming three times a week for six months — that's like 73 sessions, give or take — and I'm looking in the mirror and I don't see a damn thing different. So forgive me if I'm a little sick of "how are you doing."

**Marcus Delgado:** Seventy-three sessions. You counted. That tells me something right there — a man who quits doesn't keep score. Now tell me straight, numbers don't lie but mirrors do — what's your squat now versus week one, what's your mile time, what's your resting heart rate. You know those numbers too, or you only been counting the days you suffered?

**Renata Osei:** Marcus, easy — he's not on trial here. Trent, I actually do have some of those numbers from your check-ins, if you want them, no pressure. But I want to ask something different first — forget the mirror for a second. Has anything felt easier these past six months? Carrying groceries, stairs, sleeping, playing with your kid — anything at all?

**Trent Kowalczyk:** Look, I— yeah, okay, the stairs at my building don't wreck me anymore, I guess. And my blood pressure's down some, that's the only reason I started this whole thing in the first place. But that's not— I didn't sign up to climb stairs better, I signed up to actually look like I'm doing something. Six months, Renata, that's half a year of my life and money, and I still can't tell if any of it's real or if I'm just paying to feel good about "showing up."
